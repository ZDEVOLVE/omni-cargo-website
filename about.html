About us

Built on the A109.

Omni Cargo exists because moving cargo between Mombasa and Nairobi shouldn't feel like a gamble. We made it a schedule.

Our story

Cargo people, not middlemen.

Omni Cargo was started by a family that spent years watching traders lose time and money to the corridor — loads split across strangers' lorries, no way to know where anything was, prices that changed at the loading bay.

So we built the operation we wished existed: our own fleet of brand-new 2024 Toyota Hilux Champ pickups, our own depots at both ends, a tracking number on every load, and a price that's fixed before anything is lifted.

We're new, and that's the point — no legacy shortcuts, no "that's how it's always been done." Just the corridor, run properly.

Where we run

485 km. Six waypoints. Every day.

What we stand on

Three values. Non-negotiable.

01 · ACCOUNTABILITY

A number for every load

Nothing moves without a tracking number, a manifest entry and a photograph. If you can't check on it, we didn't do our job.

02 · CARE

Netted, not thrown

Loose cargo is personal cargo — someone's stock, someone's furniture. It's weighed, wrapped where needed, netted down and signed for.

03 · SPEED

Hours, not vibes

Under 9 hours on the corridor. Same-day in Nairobi. Quotes in 30 minutes. We put numbers on promises so you can plan around them.
