Questions

Before you book.

What counts as "medium loose cargo"?+
Anything that fits a pickup bed and doesn't need a crane: furniture, boxed stock, appliances, sacks of produce, crates, spare parts. If you're unsure, send us a photo on WhatsApp — we'll confirm in minutes.
How long does Mombasa to Nairobi take?+
Typically under 9 hours depot-to-depot on the same day. Morning pickups usually arrive in Nairobi the same evening; door delivery happens same day or next morning.
Is my cargo insured while in transit?+
Every load is manifested and photographed at pickup, and goods-in-transit cover is available for declared-value cargo. Ask for GIT cover when you book.
