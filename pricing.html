What sets the price

Four factors. That's it.

01

Route

Corridor run or within Nairobi — distance is the base of every rate.

02

Weight & volume

The band your cargo falls into. Bulky-but-light is priced by deck space.

03

Door or depot

Depot drop-off and collection is cheapest. Door-to-door adds the last mile.

04

Cover

Optional goods-in-transit cover for declared-value cargo. Small premium, big peace.
