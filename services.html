What we move

Loose cargo, defined.

We move

  • Furniture — sofas, beds, cabinets, office desks
  • Boxed shop stock and e-commerce orders
  • Appliances — fridges, cookers, TVs (packed)
  • Sacks of produce, grains and dry goods
  • Crates, spare parts and light machinery
  • Household moves that fit a Champ deck

We don't move

  • Hazardous goods — fuel, gas cylinders, chemicals
  • Livestock or perishables needing a cold chain
  • Cash, jewellery and undeclared high-value items
  • Anything above 1,000 kg on a single deck
